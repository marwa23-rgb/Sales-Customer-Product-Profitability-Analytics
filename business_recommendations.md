# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Findings
- The dataset contains 9,994 order lines across 5,009 distinct orders and 793 customers.
- Overall sales are $2.30M, profit is $286.40K, and profit margin is 12.47%.
- Tables has a negative sub-category margin of -8.56%.
- Order lines with discounts of 30% or more have a -37.32% margin, compared with 21.80% for lines below 30%.

## Recommendations
1. Review high-discount approvals and test lower discount thresholds.
2. Investigate the cost, pricing, and discount mix behind Tables and other loss-making sub-categories.
3. Prioritize product decisions using profit and margin alongside sales.
4. Segment customers by order frequency and profitability to support retention campaigns.
5. Compare regions and states by both sales and profit to avoid optimizing revenue at the expense of margin.

## Important Interpretation Note
These are descriptive findings from the supplied historical dataset. They show associations, not proof that discounts alone caused losses. Validate results by product, category, region, and customer segment before changing business policy.
